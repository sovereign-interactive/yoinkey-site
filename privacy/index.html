Privacy Policy

Yoinkey: Monkeys vs Pirates for iPhone, published by Sovereign Interactive.
Effective November 5, 2026.

This policy explains what the game collects, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean Sovereign Interactive. "The game" means Yoinkey: Monkeys vs Pirates.

The short version.

  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • The game makes a random install id on your phone. Our server and our analytics know you only by random ids.
  • Players under 13 send no analytics, are hidden from the boards, and see no community links.
  • Apple handles payments and the iCloud save. We never see your card or your Apple Account details.
  • You can turn analytics off and delete your data in Settings.
  • We do not sell your data.

What we collect and why

1. An install id

On first launch the game makes a random code (an install id) and keeps it in your save. It is not your name, your Apple Account or a device advertising id. Settings shows the start of it as your Player code, so support can find your records.

2. Your age band

On first launch the game asks for your birth year, or uses the age range Apple shares with the app when your device provides one. The game keeps only whether you are under 13 or 13 and over. It never keeps the year.

3. The game server (Ranks and Endless)

When the game goes online it creates an anonymous player on our server, with no sign-in. The server keeps:

  • your player id (a random id) and your install id;
  • when you first and last connected, and which days you played (so we can count returning players);
  • which version of a game test you were given (for example, which offer you see);
  • your board plate: a name the game makes for you from your install id, like "Lucky Coconut 8", and the cosmetics you wear on it. You cannot type a name, and the server ignores any name sent to it;
  • your Endless runs: the score, the cards you played, a recording of your moves, the game version, and the tries you used each day. The server replays each recording to check the score, and other players can watch runs from the board;
  • reports you send through Contact Support: the words you type, the game version, the screen you were on and your install id. Please do not type personal details into a report;
  • counters that limit how often your player can call the server.

We use this to run the boards, check scores, deliver purchases, answer reports and keep the game fair.

4. Purchases

Apple processes every purchase. We never see your payment card, your name or your Apple Account email. With each purchase the game gives Apple your player id, so Apple's notices name the right player. Our server receives Apple's signed record of the purchase and stores it: transaction ids, the product, the quantity, the purchase date, the price and currency when Apple includes them, and whether it was refunded or revoked. Apple also sends our server App Store notices, such as refunds, which we store. We use these records to deliver what you bought, restore it, and remove it when a purchase is refunded.

5. Play analytics (13 and over only)

If you are 13 or over and Share Analytics is on, the game sends play events to PostHog, our analytics provider, on its United States service. Events include things like: the app opened, a match started or ended (the level, result, stars, length and cards used), chests opened, the shop and offers you viewed or tapped, purchases started and how they ended, items equipped, Ranks activity, a tap on the Discord link, and error reports when something breaks (the error, where in the code it happened and the screen you were on).

Each event carries your install id, a random id for the session, the game version, your age band, how many days since install and how many sessions you have played, and a snapshot of your progress (level reached, stars, coins, diamonds, whether you own a pass or other paid items, whether you have ever bought something, and the game tests you are in).

We use analytics to understand how the game is played, find and fix bugs, and balance and improve the game. Events wait on your phone until your age is known. If you are under 13 they are thrown away and never sent.

6. Your save and iCloud

Your progress is saved on your phone. If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy in your iCloud account using Apple's iCloud storage, so your progress can follow you to another device. Apple holds that copy. Our server does not receive your save.

7. Internet connections

Any connection over the internet shares your device's IP address with the service it reaches. Our game database does not store IP addresses. Our service providers may handle them as part of running their services.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, real name, email address or phone number.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No advertising, no advertising id, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No chat or typed names in the game.

Children

We take children's privacy seriously and built the game with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in mind. When a player tells us they are under 13, the game switches to under-13 mode:

  • No analytics leave the phone, and the Share Analytics switch is not shown.
  • Their rows on the Ranks and Endless boards are hidden from other players, and their runs cannot be watched.
  • The game shows no links to Discord or other communities.
  • A grown-up must answer a sum before every purchase. Apple's Ask to Buy may also apply on a child's account.

In under-13 mode the game server still keeps the anonymous records it needs to run the game (the player id, install id, Endless runs and purchase records described above). It never holds a child's name or contact details. A parent can delete this data at any time with Delete My Data, or by contacting us. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share data only with the service providers that run the game for us, under their own terms:

  • Apple: the App Store, payments, purchase notices and iCloud storage.
  • Supabase: our game server and database.
  • PostHog: play analytics (13 and over, when Share Analytics is on).

Other players can see your board plate (the made-up name and cosmetics), your rank and score, and a replay of your best run of the week. Under-13 players are hidden.

If you choose to open the Discord link, Discord's own privacy policy applies there. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

Security

The game talks to our server and to PostHog over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database tables are locked so that only our own server functions can read or write them.

How long we keep data

  • Server records are kept while you play, until you use Delete My Data.
  • Purchase and refund records from Apple are kept for accounting and refunds. When you delete your data they are kept without your player id.
  • Analytics events are kept in PostHog to study the game over time. They are linked only to your install id.
  • Your save stays on your phone and in your iCloud until you delete it.

Your choices

  • Share Analytics (13 and over): Settings > Help & Privacy > Share Analytics. Turning it off stops all analytics and throws away any events still waiting on your phone.
  • Delete My Data: Settings > Help & Privacy > Delete My Data. This deletes your player and every server record tied to it (runs, plate, test groups, play days and reports), then your save and settings on this phone and your iCloud copy, and starts the game fresh. It cannot be undone. Purchases stay with your Apple Account, and Restore Purchases brings back the items you keep for good.
  • Delete My Data does not reach analytics events already sent to PostHog. To ask us to delete those, contact us with the Player code from Settings before you delete.
  • iCloud: you can stop the iCloud copy by turning iCloud off for the game in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes the save on your phone, but not your iCloud copy or our server records. Use Delete My Data first.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us and we will help.
