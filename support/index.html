Support

Yoinkey: Monkeys vs Pirates for iPhone, published by Sovereign Interactive.

Get help

  • In the game: tap your picture at the top of the screen, then Settings > Help & Privacy > Contact Support. Tell us what you were doing and press Send. Your report carries the game version, the screen you were on and your Player code, so we can find the problem.
  • Discord: join us at discord.gg/XeZB84938f for help, news and fixes.
  • Email: coming soon.

Settings also has For Parents, which explains what changes for players under 13, and links to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Restore purchases

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought with, and that you are online.
  2. Open the game and tap your picture at the top of the screen, then Settings.
  3. Under Save & Purchases, tap Restore Purchases. You can also find Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Shop.

Restore brings back items you keep for good. Diamonds and other items that are used up are not added again, but Restore also delivers any diamond pack you paid for that never arrived.

Moving to a new phone

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both phones. The game keeps a copy of your progress in iCloud and picks it up on the new phone. Settings > Save & Purchases shows where your progress is saved.

Delete my data

  1. Tap your picture at the top of the screen, then Settings > Help & Privacy.
  2. Tap Delete My Data (at the bottom, in red), then Delete.

This deletes your progress, coins, diamonds and items on this phone, your iCloud copy, and your records on our server (Endless runs, board plate and reports). It cannot be undone, and the game starts fresh. Purchases stay with your Apple Account: Restore Purchases brings back the items you keep for good. If our server does not answer, nothing is deleted and the game tells you, so try again when you are online. See the Privacy Policy for more.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. To ask for a refund:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
  2. Choose "Request a refund", pick the reason, then the purchase, and submit.

Apple decides every refund. If a purchase is refunded, what it gave you is removed from the game.

Turn off analytics

Players 13 and over can turn off Share Analytics in Settings > Help & Privacy. Players under 13 never send analytics.
